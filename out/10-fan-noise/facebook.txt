Some kitchens are loud because they are busy. Others are loud because the exhaust is working against itself.

Three things cause most of it. Air pushed through a duct that is too tight. A fan that has gone out of balance, usually from grease or a damaged blade. And a unit bolted straight onto the structure, so every vibration becomes building noise.

A bigger fan rarely fixes any of those. It usually makes the first two worse. We measure the route, check the fan, and treat the mounting before recommending equipment.
