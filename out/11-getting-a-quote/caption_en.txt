Most exhaust quotes are guesses dressed up as prices. Not because suppliers are careless, but because the brief was three lines and a photo of a hood.

Five things turn that around: the cooking line, the route the duct can take, the building and its access, the roof outlet, and how soon you need it. With those, a supplier can price the steel, the fan and the labour without inventing allowances.

It also protects you. A quote built on a real route has fewer variations later, when the duct meets a beam nobody mentioned.
