A kitchen exhaust is half of a pair. The fan pulls air out, and something has to replace it.

When replacement air is missing, the symptoms look like fan problems. Doors are hard to open. Smoke hangs at head height. The flame pulls away from the burner. Grease and smells drift into the dining room instead of leaving the building.

The usual mistake is to fit a bigger fan. That makes the pressure worse. The right fix is a planned path for incoming air, sized to what the hood extracts.
