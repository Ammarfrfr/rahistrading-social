Most weak extractors are not weak fans. They are long, twisting ducts that nobody added up before the fan was chosen.

Air does not care about the straight line on the drawing. It follows the real path: up, across, around a column, through a narrow section, then out over the roof. Each of those turns takes pressure the fan has to pay for.

If the kitchen already exists, we measure the path as built and match the fan to it. If it is still on paper, we redraw the route while it is still cheap to change.
