Stainless or GI. Both belong in a kitchen exhaust system, just not in the same place.

Where grease, steam and wash water are constant, stainless steel is the honest choice. It resists rust and survives being cleaned every single day. Where the duct is dry, long and simply carrying smoke out of the building, coated GI does the job at a lower cost.

The mistake is picking one material for the whole system. We work with every steel type and specify each section on its own.
